how to play ccgameo

  • in tab {clicker} you click the black space

  • on the bottom of your screen youll see your stats your current coins, coins per click, upgrade tokens per click, tokens and user and coins per second

  • to purchase a upgrade token youll go to the tab {upgrades} at the bottom theres a button that says "upgrade tokens"

  • with the upgrade tokens you can purchase card tokens by pressing the + buttom

  • with those card tokens you can purchase upgrades each upgrade requires a certain amount of card tokens
